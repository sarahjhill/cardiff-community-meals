How it works

Cardiff Community Meals exists to make sure nobody in the city goes without a hot meal while they're recovering from illness, living with a disability, or newly home from hospital — inspired by services like Cardiff Council's Meals on Wheels, with an added layer of community donation and local business involvement.

Welsh first, open to all

We lead in Welsh because this is Wales, and language is part of how we care for each other. But our door — and our table — is open to absolutely anyone who needs it or wants to help, whoever you are and wherever you started out. That's not a contradiction. That's just Cardiff.

1

Request or refer

Submit a request for yourself, or refer someone else — with their consent. Every request is reviewed before it's published; third-party referrals are checked with the recipient directly, or come from a recognised source such as a GP, hospital discharge team, or Age Connects Cardiff & Vale.

2

Donate & book

Community members fund one meal, a week, or more, and choose a local business to cook it. Only a first name and general area are ever shown publicly — never a full name or exact address.

3

Cook, deliver, track

Our partner businesses prepare and deliver the meal, updating its status along the way. Everyone — donor, recipient, and the public impact page — can see it through to delivery.

Keeping people safe

Because the people we support are, by definition, vulnerable, safeguarding sits at the centre of the platform, not at the edge of it:

Verified before public

No request is visible to donors until an admin has reviewed it. Referrals made on someone else's behalf are confirmed with them directly wherever possible.

Privacy by default

Public listings show a first name and general area only. Full contact and address details are only ever shared with the business fulfilling a booking.

Vetted businesses

Partner businesses must hold a valid Food Standards Agency hygiene rating, checked at sign-up and periodically thereafter.

We handle personal data in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. If a delivery ever raises a welfare concern, our admins have a direct escalation path to Cardiff Council Adult Services.

Frequently asked questions

Is this affiliated with Cardiff Council?

No — this is an independent community pilot, designed to complement existing services like Cardiff Council's Meals on Wheels, not replace them. If you need immediate statutory support, Cardiff Council can be reached on 029 2053 7080.

Is my donation guaranteed to reach someone?

Yes — donations are tied to a specific, verified request, or can go to a general fund matched to the next verified request in your chosen area.

How do businesses get paid?

In a full production build, donor payments would be held until delivery is confirmed, then released to the business — protecting both the donor and the business partner.
