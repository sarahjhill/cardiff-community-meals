How it works

Three simple steps, with a verification check built in at every stage to protect the people we're helping.

1

Request or refer

Anyone can request help for themselves, or refer a family member, neighbour, or patient. Every request is checked before it's made public — no one is listed without consent.

2

Donate & book

Community members browse open requests, choose how many meals to fund, and pick (or let us match) a local business to cook them.

3

Cook, deliver, track

The business prepares and delivers the meal. Every step is tracked, right through to confirmed delivery — for the donor, the recipient, and the platform's public impact record.
